## Document upload portal

**Controller:** [Firm name], [address], [tax number].
Contact for privacy questions: [email].

**What data we process.** When you use the portal, we process:
- your name and the email address(es) the firm registered for you;
- the documents you send and any note you write;
- the date, time and case of each upload;
- a security log of sign-in activity (code requests and sign-in attempts), where your email is stored only as a one-way hash.

**Why, and on what legal basis.**
- Receiving and organising the documents for your case: performance of the contract for legal services, or pre-contractual steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Meeting legal and professional record-keeping obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- Protecting the portal against abuse: the firm's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

**Where the data is kept.** In the firm's Google [Workspace] account (Google Drive, Google Sheets and Gmail). Google acts as processor under the [Google Workspace Data Processing Addendum]. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards that agreement provides.

**Client management with an AI assistant.** [If applicable:] The firm uses Claude (Anthropic) to register clients and cases in the portal. In that case, your name, email and the case name are sent to Anthropic, acting as [processor]. The documents you upload are not shared with Anthropic.

**How long.**
- Documents and the upload log are kept for as long as your case and the applicable legal periods require: [state].
- The security log is deleted after 90 days.
- Access codes expire after 10 minutes, and sessions after 45 minutes without activity.

**Cookies.** The portal uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It only remembers your language choice in your browser. Google may use technical cookies to serve the page.

**Your rights.** You can ask for access to, correction or erasure of your data, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. To do so, contact [email]. You can also complain to the data protection authority (in Portugal, CNPD, www.cnpd.pt).
